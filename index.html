Who is Youngjae Choi?

Research Interest

  • Computer Vision & Deep Learning.
  • Image Generation / Motion Generation.
  • Embodied AI & Autonomous Driving.
I am an AI engineer at Motifdrive, an autonomous vehicle start-up, and a Master's student in the Department of Digital Media at Soongsil University, working in the Reality Lab under Prof. Heewon Kim.
My research focuses on multimodal generative models — generating fine-grained images and expressive human motions from text inputs, with methods that reflect detailed semantics and emotional context in the generation process.

At Motifdrive, I research generative AI for synthetic data generation and augmentation in end-to-end autonomous driving, develop VLM-based data management and scenario mining frameworks, and contribute to 3D object detection research using multi-modal sensor data.
