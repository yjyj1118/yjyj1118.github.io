About

I am an AI engineer at Motifdrive, an autonomous vehicle start-up, and an M.S. student in the Department of Digital Media at Soongsil University, working in the Reality Lab advised by Prof. Heewon Kim.

My research focuses on multimodal generative models — generating fine-grained images and expressive human motions from text, with methods that reflect detailed semantics and emotional context in the generation process.

At Motifdrive, I work on generative AI for synthetic data generation and augmentation in end-to-end autonomous driving, VLM-based data management and scenario mining frameworks, and 3D object detection with multi-modal sensor data.
